# Apple Financial Statements Workbook

This workbook presents Apple Inc.’s principal financial statements in a compact, analysis-friendly format. The three worksheets described below provide complementary views of the company’s financial position, operating performance, and movements in cash. Amounts are generally shown in millions of dollars unless otherwise noted.

## Balance Sheet

The **Balance Sheet** worksheet presents Apple’s condensed consolidated financial position as of September 27, 2025 and September 28, 2024. Assets are separated into current and non-current categories, including cash and cash equivalents, marketable securities, receivables, inventories, property and equipment, and other assets. Liabilities are similarly divided between current and non-current obligations, covering accounts payable, deferred revenue, commercial paper, term debt, and other liabilities. The worksheet then presents shareholders’ equity, including common stock and additional paid-in capital, accumulated deficit, and accumulated other comprehensive loss. Subtotals show current assets, non-current assets, current liabilities, non-current liabilities, total liabilities, and total shareholders’ equity. The final line confirms that total assets equal total liabilities and shareholders’ equity. A check section on the right compares selected 2025 calculated totals with expected amounts and reports whether each balance-sheet relationship is correct.

## Income Statement

The **Income Statement** worksheet summarizes Apple’s operating results for fiscal years 2023, 2024, and 2025, with amounts shown in millions of dollars except per-share data. It begins with revenue and cost of goods and services sold, producing gross profit. Research and development and selling, general, and administrative expenses are then presented as the main operating-expense categories. These items lead to operating income, followed by non-operating income or expense, income before taxes, the income-tax provision, and net income. The worksheet also includes basic and diluted earnings per share, together with the related weighted-average share counts. Two parallel sections are provided: “Pure XLOOKUP,” which retrieves reported figures using XBRL tags, and “XLOOKUP + Calculations,” which combines retrieved inputs with calculated subtotals. This side-by-side presentation helps users compare directly sourced financial-statement values with results derived from the underlying components.

## Cash Flows

The **Cash Flows** worksheet presents Apple’s consolidated cash flows for fiscal years 2023, 2024, and 2025 in millions of dollars. It starts with beginning cash, cash equivalents, and restricted cash, then organizes cash movements into operating, investing, and financing activities. Operating activities begin with net income and adjust for depreciation and amortization, share-based compensation, deferred taxes, other non-cash items, and changes in operating assets and liabilities. Investing activities include purchases, maturities, and sales of marketable securities, capital expenditures, and other investing transactions. Financing activities cover tax withholding on equity awards, dividends, common-stock repurchases, term-debt issuances and repayments, commercial paper, and other financing items. Section totals show the cash generated or used by each activity category. The worksheet concludes with the annual increase or decrease in cash and the ending cash balance, providing a complete reconciliation from beginning to ending cash for each year.
